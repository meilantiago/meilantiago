### 🔬 Overview

Undergraduate Physicist graduating in 2027 (University of Buenos Aires) with a primary orientation in **applied probability, statistics, and numerical modeling**. 

My work bridges first-principles physical theory with production-grade engineering software:
- **Subsurface & Energy Systems**: Full-cycle domain tooling across drilling mechanics (MSE, stick-slip, SPE 92194), 1D geomechanical earth models (poroelasticity, hydraulic fracturing sweet-spotting), and physics-informed decline curve forecasting in Vaca Muerta.
- **Embedded Systems & Instrumentation**: Real-time closed-loop motion control firmware, Field-Oriented Control (FOC), high-resolution magnetic encoder linearization, and sensor telemetry on microcontrollers (RP2040, C/C++).
- **Scientific Computing & ML**: Statistical signal processing, nonlinear optimization, hybrid physics-ML residual models, and automated data pipelines.
